The `customer_id` identifies a customer record, while `customer_unique_id` identifies a customer across records and is not unique in this dataset. Therefore, `customer_unique_id` is more appropriate for counting distinct customers. The `order_items` table is a junction table that resolves the many-to-many relationship between orders and products, while also recording seller and item-level details. The customer-to-order data is stricter than the general business relationship because each customer record has exactly one order in this dataset, although a customer may place multiple orders in a general business model. A zero-or-many relationship between orders and order items is justified because 775 orders have no associated item rows.
